🛍️ Vrinda Store Annual Sales Report 2022

📊 Excel Dashboard | Data Cleaning | Data Analysis | Insights



## 📘 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to help Vrinda Store analyze its 2022 annual sales performance and identify actionable insights to improve sales in 2023.
By analyzing data related to orders, customers, sales channels, and product categories, this dashboard enables Vrinda to make data-driven business decisions.
## 🧾 Data Dictionary

| **Column Name**      | **Description**                        | **Example**                      |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Order ID**         | Unique identifier for each order       | OID12345                         |
| **Order Date**       | Date when the order was placed         | 12-03-2022                       |
| **Customer ID**      | Unique customer identifier             | CUST567                          |
| **Gender**           | Gender of the customer                 | Male / Female                    |
| **Age**              | Age of the customer                    | 34                               |
| **Age Group**        | Grouped age range of customer          | Adult (30–49)                    |
| **State**            | State from which the order was placed  | Maharashtra                      |
| **Category**         | Type of product purchased              | Ethnic Dress                     |
| **Channel**          | Online platform where order was placed | Amazon                           |
| **Order Status**     | Delivery status of the order           | Delivered / Returned / Cancelled |
| **Sales Amount**     | Total amount of sale in INR            | 1500                             |
| **Quantity Ordered** | Total items ordered                    | 3                                |
| **Month**            | Month derived from Order Date          | March                            |

## 🎯 Objective

Vrinda Store wants to create an annual sales report for 2022 so that the company can better understand its customers and improve sales performance in 2023.
## 💡 Key Questions Solved

1.Compare Sales and Orders in one chart

2.Which month recorded the highest sales and orders?

3.Who purchased more — men or women?

4.What are the different order statuses?

5.Which are the top 10 states contributing to sales?

6.Relationship between age group and gender based on orders

7.Which channel (Amazon, Flipkart, etc.) contributed the most sales?

8.Which is the highest selling category?
## 🧩 Project Steps
### 1️⃣ Data Cleaning
- Removed blanks, duplicates, and nulls  
- Corrected spelling and standardized formats  
- Fixed date fields and ensured category consistency  

### 2️⃣ Data Processing
- Added calculated columns: **Month**, **Age Group**  
- Merged data for unified analysis  

### 3️⃣ Data Analysis
- Built pivot tables for **Sales Trend**, **Gender**, **State**, **Channel**, and **Age Group** insights  

### 4️⃣ Dashboard Creation
- Designed an **interactive Excel dashboard** using slicers and charts (combo, pie, and bar)

## Dashboard Highlights:

Women contributed ~65% of total purchases

Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are top 3 states (~35%)

Adult age group (30–49 yrs) contributed ~50%

Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra together contributed ~80% of total sales
## 🧠 Insights

Women are more likely to buy compared to men (~65%)

Top performing states: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh

Adult age group (30–49 yrs) is the primary buyer segment (~50%)

Online channels like Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are major revenue contributors (~80%)
